        Attack rating by infusion

        Select an infusion to see where its damage comes from. Numbers are rounded down, like the game's status screen. AR adds up every damage type, but enemies defend against each type separately, so split damage (physical plus an element) usually lands for a little less than its total suggests.

        Weapon data: Elden Ring patch {{PATCH}} game params (base damage, upgrade multipliers, stat curves, damage-type scaling and status effects) as extracted by Tom Clark's open-source Elden Ring weapon calculator (MIT license). Results are cross-checked against eldenring.tclark.io.

        Boss data: New Game HP, defense, damage negation and status resistance of {{BOSS_COUNT}} boss fights from the Elden Ring Wiki, whose combat tables come from the game files through nex3's BossDataExtractor. Status proc damage follows the wiki's Hemorrhage, Frostbite, Poison and Scarlet Rot pages.

        Movesets: motion values of {{MOVESET_COUNT}} weapons from the community Elden Ring Motion Values and Attack Data sheet (patch 1.16), and each attack's damage type, timing and stamina cost from frame data read from the game files (patch {{TIMING_PATCH}}) by AtomechLLC's game-animation-library, which follows sovietspaceship's Elden Ring Frame Data Explorer.

        Talismans: effects of {{TALISMAN_COUNT}} talismans from the Elden Ring Wiki and Eldenpedia. How much room each boss leaves to attack is this calculator's own estimate, not game data.

        Icons: the in-game icons of {{ICON_COUNT}} weapons and talismans, FromSoftware's artwork, as shown on Eldenpedia's item pages.
